Privacy Policy

How we protect and handle your data

For information regarding terms of service and user responsibilities, please review our Terms & Conditions.

Data Collection

SpotScrape collects minimal data to provide our services:

  • Account Information: Username, email, and encrypted password for registered users
  • Usage Data: Spotify URLs you extract and search history saved in your account
  • Technical Data: Basic browser headers necessary for HTTP communication

Data Usage

We use your data strictly to:

  • Provide Spotify metadata extraction services
  • Maintain your optional personal extraction history and statistics
  • Deliver synchronized lyrics and media embed links

Data Protection

Your privacy is protected:

  • Encryption: Passwords are hashed using SHA-256
  • No Sharing: We never sell or share your personal data with third parties
  • Local Storage: History is tied to your account and stored in your local SQLite database

Third-Party Services

When using SpotScrape, the following external interactions occur:

  • Spotify: Public web pages are queried for metadata, and Spotify embed iframes stream preview clips directly from Spotify servers.
  • YouTube: Video search queries are dispatched to locate matching official music videos.
  • Lyrics Providers: Synchronized lyrics are queried via open lyric sources through `syncedlyrics`.

User Rights & Data Deletion

You retain 100% control over your data. You can clear your extraction history anytime or permanently delete your account through Account Settings.
