Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about extracting Spotify metadata, streaming embeds, and synced lyrics.

SpotScrape is a modern, fast, and completely free web application designed to extract rich Spotify metadata (tracks, albums, playlists, artists, and podcasts) with synced lyrics and streaming player embeds.
How to use:
Simply paste any public Spotify link into the search box on the Home page, or search by song/artist keywords on the Search Catalog page.

Yes, SpotScrape is 100% free forever. There are no subscriptions, no premium tiers, no forced registrations, and no artificial rate limits. You can explore unlimited tracks, albums, playlists, and lyrics at any time.

SpotScrape integrates automated lyrics retrieval that searches for synchronized and plain-text lyrics for any extracted song.
  • Instant Preview: Click the Lyrics button to view full formatted lyrics inside our responsive modal.
  • .LRC Format (Synced): Download synchronized lyrics files with precise timestamp tags ([mm:ss.xx]) ready for music players like VLC, Apple Music, or Kodi.
  • .TXT Format (Plain): Download clean, plain text lyrics without timestamps for reading or printing.

SpotScrape allows you to preview and enjoy audio and video directly within the app without leaving the site:
  • Spotify Player: Click Spotify Player to open an in-browser official Spotify embed iframe that streams 30-second previews or full tracks for logged-in Spotify listeners.
  • YouTube Player: Click YouTube Video to open a fullscreen privacy-enhanced (youtube-nocookie) embed of the matching official music video.

We believe in maximum privacy and simplicity. SpotScrape runs completely stateless:
  • We do not store your search history, credentials, or personal queries in any database.
  • URL Query Driven: Your searches and extracts are reflected in the browser address bar (e.g. /?url=... or /search-page?q=...).
  • Refresh Safe: Refreshing the page or sharing the link directly with friends preserves your exact extracted data without losing state.

SpotScrape supports all standard Spotify media URLs:
Tracks Individual songs with lyrics & previews
Albums Full album tracklists & release dates
Playlists Complete public playlist tracks
Artists Artist biographies, followers & top tracks
Podcasts Shows & episode metadata with descriptions

Yes! Every extraction includes a one-click Download JSON button. The JSON file contains clean, formatted fields (title, artist, artwork image URLs, durations in seconds and MM:SS, release dates, track URLs, and YouTube links).

SpotScrape operates solely as a client-side tool and metadata viewer for publicly indexed information. All data and lyrics retrieved or downloaded by the user are the sole responsibility of the user. SpotScrape does not host or distribute copyrighted music audio files. For more details, review our Terms & Conditions.

If an extraction fails, verify that:
  • The Spotify URL is public (private or hidden playlists cannot be accessed).
  • The URL format matches open.spotify.com/track/... (or album, playlist, artist, episode).
  • The content is not blocked in your geographic region.
