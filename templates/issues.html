Troubleshooting & Issue Center

Issue & Help Desk

Encountered an issue while extracting songs, playlists, or lyrics? Here are instant solutions and diagnostic tools.

Spotify Link Not Extracting

If a link gives an extraction error, verify that the Spotify playlist or album is public. Private links or user collaborative playlists without public sharing cannot be scraped.

Fix: Open Spotify → Share → Copy Link to ensure proper format.
Lyrics Not Found

Lyrics are retrieved automatically across major synced lyrical registries. Obscure instrumental tracks or very recently released songs may not yet have synchronized LRC lyrics.

Fix: Try searching for the main release track title without remix/extended mix tags.
Spotify / YouTube Embed Blocked

Some strict ad-blockers or browser privacy extensions block embedded iframes from open.spotify.com or youtube-nocookie.com.

Fix: Click "Open in Spotify" or "Open on YouTube" on the modal header to stream directly.
Region-Restricted Content

Some Spotify songs are licensed exclusively in specific countries and may show empty metadata in unsupported territories.

Fix: Use universal search to locate international releases of the song.

Report a Problem

Submit details about a broken link, parsing bug, or missing lyrics so we can improve SpotScrape.
